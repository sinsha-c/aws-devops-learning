## About

Senior DevOps Engineer with **8+ years of hands-on experience** designing, automating, and scaling production infrastructure at Amagi Corporation — a global leader in cloud broadcast and streaming technology.

My career has been built on a foundation of **infrastructure reliability, cloud-native architecture, and engineering automation** — translating complex operational problems into clean, repeatable solutions. I've worked across the full infrastructure lifecycle: from provisioning cloud environments with Terraform and Ansible, to building CI/CD pipelines in Jenkins, to managing Kubernetes workloads and ensuring production uptime through proactive monitoring.

After a deliberate career break, I'm back — and actively rebuilding with a focus on where cloud and DevOps are heading next.

> *"Infrastructure should be invisible when it's working well. Getting there requires everything but invisibility."*

## Building in Public: DevOps Comeback Journey

> *A senior engineer returns. Here's the honest account of what that looks like.*

After years delivering production infrastructure at scale for a global media technology company, I stepped away. Life happens — and sometimes the most professional thing you can do is step back, reassess, and return with intention.

What I found when I came back: the fundamentals haven't changed, but the ecosystem around them has evolved significantly. The tools I built on — Terraform, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines — are now the baseline. GitOps is standard. Observability is a discipline. Platform engineering is redefining what it means to be a DevOps practitioner.

So I'm doing what engineers do when faced with a gap: I'm closing it systematically, in public, with evidence.

**Every project in this GitHub is part of that process.** Not curated highlights — actual learning in progress. Working code. Documented decisions. Iterative improvement. The same discipline I applied to production infrastructure, applied now to rebuilding at the frontier.

This is what a comeback looks like when you approach it like an engineer.
